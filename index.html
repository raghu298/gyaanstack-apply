How it works

Paste a posting. Review. Send. Prepare.

  1. Profile onceExperience, projects and skills, or import an existing resume.
  2. Paste the jobThe app lists the requirements and checks each against your profile.
  3. Review the draftsEdit the resume and letter by hand or in a chat, with every version kept.
  4. Send and trackDownload PDFs or send from Gmail; the board follows each application.
  5. PrepareAt the interview stage, practise the questions picked for that job.

Honest drafts

It writes from your profile and nothing else.

A skill the posting asks for that you have not listed is marked as a gap, not added to your resume. Add it to your profile if it is true, and every later application can use it. Nothing is sent or deleted until you confirm.

Privacy

You decide what leaves your computer.

  • Runs where you put it. Your laptop or a small server of your own. One person, one password.
  • Local models work. With Ollama or LM Studio, your profile and documents never leave your hardware.
  • Google is optional. Gmail and Calendar stay off until you connect them; every send needs your confirmation.
  • Your data, your copy. Export everything as one file, keep nightly backups, or delete it all with one button.
