How it works

From a job posting to a sent application in four steps.

You write your background once. After that, each new job takes a paste and a review.

  1. Build your profile

    Add experience, projects, skills, education and certifications. You can import an existing resume (PDF, Word or Markdown) to fill it in faster.

  2. Paste a posting

    The app reads the job description, lists its requirements and checks each one against your profile.

  3. Review the drafts

    You get a resume and cover letter for that job. Change them in a chat, edit any part by hand, or roll back to an earlier version.

  4. Send and track

    Download the PDFs or send from Gmail. Each application moves across a board from draft to offer.

Honest drafts

It writes from your profile and nothing else.

When a posting asks for a skill you have not listed, the app marks it as a gap. It does not add the skill to your resume. You can fill the gap in your profile if it is true, and every later application can use it.

  • Every AI edit can be undone, and each document keeps its versions.
  • Nothing is sent or deleted until you confirm it.
  • Requirements show where in your profile they were found.

Features

The whole job search in one app.

Searching, writing, sending, replies and interviews all sit next to each application.

Find jobs

Search JSearch (LinkedIn, Indeed and more), Adzuna and FreeHire from one screen, plus startups that are hiring from YC and Hacker News.

Job agent

Runs your saved searches once a day, scores new postings against your profile and can prepare drafts for you to review.

Gmail and Calendar

Optional. Send each application with a short email from your own address, see replies on the application, and keep interviews in your calendar.

Board and dashboard

Every application on a board and in a table, with a funnel, a week-by-week chart and your next interviews.

Themes and languages

Twelve resume themes and a theme builder, in 21 languages, printed to PDF on your machine.

Daily GenAI job run

Optional script for GenAI roles in India: it searches every morning, emails postings that ask for it with your own resume, and mails you a summary.

Privacy

You decide what leaves your computer.

There is no GyaanStack account and no server in the middle. Your data stays in your own database, and the app talks only to the services you switch on. Read the privacy policy.

Runs where you put it

Your laptop, a phone browser on your network, or a small server on your own domain. One person, one password.

Local models work

With Ollama or LM Studio, your profile and documents never leave your hardware. A hosted model receives the text it writes from.

Google is optional

Gmail and Calendar stay off until you connect them. Every send needs your confirmation in the app.

Your data, your copy

Export everything as one file, keep nightly backups, or delete it all with one button.

Install

Running on your own computer.

Desktop app
