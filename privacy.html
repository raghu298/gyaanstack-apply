Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

GyaanStack Apply is a self-hosted job-application app published by GyaanStack. Each copy runs on its owner's own computer or server and is used by one person. GyaanStack does not run a central service for it and does not receive your data.

The GyaanStack install of the app connects to a single Google account, the account of the person who runs it. If you install the app yourself, you connect it with your own Google OAuth client, and this policy describes what the app does with that access.

What it accesses in Google

  • Gmail (read): to find replies to job applications and link each one to the matching application.
  • Gmail (send): to send job applications, with their resume and cover letter PDFs, and replies to employers. Each send is confirmed by the owner, or comes from the owner's own daily auto-apply script.
  • Google Calendar: to show interview times, find free slots and add interviews to the owner's calendar.

The app asks only for the permissions of the features the owner turns on. It does not read attachments in received mail.

Where your data goes

The profile, applications, documents and the mail linked to applications are stored in a database on the owner's own computer or server. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

The only exception is the AI provider the owner picks in settings. When the app writes a document or reads an employer's reply, it sends that text to that provider. With a local model (Ollama or LM Studio), the text stays on the owner's hardware.

Google refresh tokens and API keys are stored encrypted. They are never sent to the browser or written to logs.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or with Disconnect in the app's Google settings. Delete everything in the app's data settings removes all stored data and revokes Google access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

GyaanStack Apply's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail and Calendar data is used only to provide the features described above, is not used for advertising, and is not used to train AI models.
